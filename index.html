About

I am doing my PhD at Northeastern University being advised by Weiyan Shi looking at agent swarms and managing agentic systems. Before this, I was a Research Scientist at ASAPP building LLM reliable agents for customer service.
I received my Masters in Computer Science and Engineering with a focus on AI from the University of Michigan in 2022. Prior to that in 2020 I received a B.S. in Computer Science and B.S. in Mathematics from Virginia Tech.
